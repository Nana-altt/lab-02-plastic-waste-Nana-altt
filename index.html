Why it matters

Plastic waste is one of the most visible environmental challenges today. Most single-use plastics take hundreds of years to decompose, and some never fully disappear.

Bring a bottle

Replace disposable bottles with a reusable one to cut waste dramatically.

Carry a bag

Keep reusable shopping bags in your bag to avoid plastic checkout bags.

Refuse straws

Say no to single-use plastic straws and cutlery whenever possible.
